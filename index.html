Interpret carefully

What the simulator means

The simulator shows a simplified educational pathway: viral activity and immune activation may contribute to blood–brain barrier stress, microglial activation, and neuroinflammation.

What it does not mean

What the simulator cannot do

It cannot diagnose HIV-associated neurocognitive disorders, predict symptoms, estimate an individual’s risk, recommend treatment, or replace qualified medical care.

About this project

From HIV reservoir research to digital science communication

I built NeuroImmune Map as a student educational project to explore how concepts from HIV reservoir and neuroimmune research can be communicated through an interactive digital model. The equations and values are deliberately illustrative and transparent, not validated biomedical or clinical calculations.
