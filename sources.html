Evidence and Ethical Considerations

The NeuroImmune Map is a tool designed to visualize the complex interactions between the nervous and immune systems. The information presented in this map is based on a comprehensive review of scientific literature, including peer-reviewed articles, clinical studies, and authoritative sources in the fields of neuroscience and immunology. The slider values are not patient data or clinically validated predictions, they are just illustrative values I invented so it could help me and others visualize the interactions between the nervous and immune systems.

This was solely made for educational purposes and is not in any way medical advice.

Limitations

  • Scientific research changes, and this website is not a complete clinical reference.
  • Findings from a study may not apply equally to every person living with HIV.
  • Association between biological markers and symptoms does not automatically establish direct causation.
  • This project does not collect health information or make clinical predictions.
